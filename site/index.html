ENOUGH TO BE USEFUL

A helpful little toolkit.

The essentials for small teams, without building your day around a video platform.

01

Record, then review.

Screen and microphone, a countdown you can hear, pause and resume. Review locally before publishing.

02

Send a little context.

Shareable links, optional passcodes, and a message that includes what your recipient needs to watch.

03

Find the right words.

Optional AI transcripts and title suggestions. Review and edit them before sharing; AI can make mistakes.

04

Keep your team in view.

A library, owner controls, team roles, simple playback trends, and a first-login guide. Light or dark, as you like.

BRING YOUR OWN CLOUDFLARE

A small app.
A real deployment.

You'll configure a Worker, a private R2 bucket, D1, a custom hostname, and Cloudflare Access for your team. AI is optional to use.

You pay your providers directly. Check their current prices and limits; we don't promise zero-cost hosting.
